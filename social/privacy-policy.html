FactureHub Privacy Policy

This explains what FactureHub collects, why, who it's shared with, and what you can do about it. It covers the FactureHub platform specifically, separate from the Facture Discord community.

Information We Collect

Account information: your email address, a securely hashed password (or, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile info Google provides — your name, email, and profile picture), and your username.

Profile information: whatever you choose to add — display name, bio, pronouns, gender identity, profile picture, and banner image.

Content: posts, comments, community notes, direct messages, group chat messages, and any images, GIFs, or video you attach to them.

Account security: if you enable two-factor authentication, the authenticator secret is held directly by our authentication provider (Supabase) and is never visible to us or stored in our own database.

Signup IP address: recorded once at account creation and used only to detect and limit the number of accounts created from the same network, as a defense against ban evasion and spam accounts. It isn't tied to your activity afterward.

Local device storage: we use your browser's local storage for things like your theme preference and a timestamp of your last human-verification check — none of this leaves your device or is sent to us.

Signing In With Google

If you choose "Continue with Google" instead of creating a password, we request only your basic Google profile: your name, email address, and profile picture. We use this solely to create and sign you into your FactureHub account — to pre-fill your display name and profile picture, and to confirm it's really you on future logins.

We don't request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, or any other Google service or data beyond that basic profile. We don't use this information for advertising, and we never share it with third parties outside the service providers listed below. You can revoke FactureHub's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and you can still delete your FactureHub account afterward from Settings as described under Data Retention below.

How We Use Information

To create and secure your account, show your profile and content to other members as intended, deliver direct messages and notifications, enforce our Terms of Service through warnings, suspensions, and appeals, and detect and limit abuse such as ban evasion through alt accounts. We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Third-Party Services

FactureHub is built on a small number of infrastructure providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — handles account authentication, our database, and file storage for images and video. This is where your account, content, and (if enabled) two-factor authentication secret actually live.
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google instead of a password, Google handles that authentication and shares your basic profile with us.
  • Cloudflare — provides the human-verification check (Turnstile) you may see, our content delivery, and basic uptime/traffic protection.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices covering how they process data on our behalf.

Data Sharing

We don't sell personal information. Outside of the service providers listed above, we only share information when required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request, or when necessary to investigate a Terms of Service violation.

Data Retention

Deactivating your account hides your profile until you log back in; nothing is deleted. Requesting account deletion from Settings schedules permanent removal after 30 days, giving you a window to change your mind by logging back in before everything’s gone for good. Content removed as part of a moderation action is kept on file alongside the report that led to it, so a decision can still be reviewed on appeal.

Children's Privacy

FactureHub requires you to be at least 13 years old, as stated in our Terms of Service. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger, and accounts found to belong to someone under 13 are terminated.

Your Rights

We try to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act (KCDPA), which gives you the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Most of this you can already do yourself: edit your profile and settings directly, or delete your account from Settings. For anything else, including a full data export or a question about what we hold, contact us as described below.

Changes To This Policy

We'll update this page as FactureHub changes. Continued use of FactureHub after an update means you've accepted the current version, whether or not you went back and reread it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request can be sent through a support ticket in our Discord server.
