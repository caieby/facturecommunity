Project NEXUS · Community Intelligence

NEXUS helps server owners and members spot known bad actors before they cause harm. Our staff review reports and evidence, then add confirmed offenders to a shared database that anyone can check from any server NEXUS is in, or from your DMs.

— Servers Protected
— Flagged Accounts

🔍 Check any user (/check)

See whether a user is flagged, along with their risk level (Clear, Low, Moderate, High or Critical) and NEXUS ID.

View their known accounts and alts, the servers associated with what they did, and the reason they were flagged.

See their linked Roblox accounts and the Roblox communities they own.

Or right-click any user and choose Apps → NEXUS Check.

🚩 Report a user (/report)

Step-by-step instructions for submitting a report with verified video evidence to our staff.

⚖️ Appeal a record (/appeal)

Flagged and think it's a mistake? Explain your side and share evidence. Staff review every appeal, and you'll get the decision by DM.

🔒 Private by design

Every command reply is visible only to you.

Evidence is visible only to NEXUS staff.

🛡️ Built to follow Discord's rules

NEXUS doesn't read your messages, track activity or scrape member lists.

It needs no privileged intents and only 4 basic permissions.

Every record is added by hand by staff, backed by evidence.

📲 Works everywhere

Add NEXUS to your server, or to your account to use it in any DM or group chat.
