Privacy Policy

This explains what Project Nexus collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Overview

Project Nexus is a Discord bot built and operated by Facture. This policy covers the bot itself and this website. By inviting Project Nexus to a server, using its commands, or interacting with a server it operates in, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

To provide its features, Project Nexus processes information made available to it through the Discord API, including user IDs, usernames, server and channel IDs, role information, and message content in servers where it's present. Message content is processed for moderation and security purposes, such as detecting rule violations or threats to a server's safety.

We do not collect payment information, email addresses, or IP addresses through the bot. This website's own age verification uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which is covered separately by Cloudflare's own privacy practices.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate Project Nexus's moderation and security features, enforce the rules a server's administrators configure, respond to commands, maintain the reliability of the bot, and investigate abuse or violations of these policies. We don't use collected information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Data Retention

Logs and flagged content are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for moderation, security, and abuse investigation purposes, and are deleted on a routine basis after that. We may retain specific records longer where required by law, to resolve an active dispute or investigation, or to enforce our agreements.

Data Sharing

We don't sell personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us host or operate Project Nexus, bound by confidentiality obligations, or where disclosure is required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request from law enforcement.

Server Administrator Responsibility

Administrators who add Project Nexus to a server and configure its features are responsible for operating their server in line with applicable law, including informing their members of any monitoring or moderation tools in use where required.

Children's Privacy

Project Nexus is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under Discord's own minimum age requirement, or a higher age where required by local law. We don't knowingly collect information from children in violation of applicable law.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information Project Nexus holds about you by contacting us through our support server. Removing Project Nexus from a server stops further collection in that server, though some records may be retained as described under Data Retention above.

Legal Compliance

Project Nexus is operated in compliance with applicable data protection law and Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy. We cooperate with valid legal requests from law enforcement and take action against unlawful use of the bot.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted here, and continued use of Project Nexus after a change means you accept the update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data request can be sent through a support ticket in our Discord server.
