Description Of Service

Project Nexus is a Discord bot, operated by Facture through the Discord API, built around a shared database of confirmed bad actors. Server members and owners can use /check to look up a user's risk level, known alts, associated servers, flag reason, and linked Roblox accounts. /report lets you submit a report with evidence for staff to review, and /appeal lets a flagged user dispute a record. Nexus works both as a server bot and as a user app you can use in your own DMs and group chats. Features and commands may change, be added, or be removed over time.

Reports And The Nexus Database

Submitting a report through /report means affirming the information and evidence you provide is accurate and genuinely yours to share. Our staff review every report and its evidence before any record is added to the database, no one is added automatically. Submitting a false, fabricated, or misleading report, or evidence you don't have the right to share, is a violation of these terms.

Consequence: Loss of access to Nexus's reporting features, and the report itself will be disregarded.

Appeals

A user who believes they were flagged in error can dispute the record through /appeal by explaining their side and providing evidence. Staff review every appeal and deliver their decision by DM. Submitting repeated bad-faith appeals with no new information may result in that user's appeal access being limited.
