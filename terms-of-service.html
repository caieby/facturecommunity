Privacy and Consumer Rights

Facture is committed to protecting the privacy of its members and handling personal information responsibly. We strive to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Kentucky Consumer Data Protection Act (KCDPA).

The KCDPA is a Kentucky privacy law that grants consumers certain rights regarding their personal data. These rights may include the ability to access personal information, request corrections, request deletion of personal data, and opt out of certain types of data processing, such as targeted advertising or the sale of personal information, where applicable.

Facture will take reasonable measures to safeguard personal information and honor applicable privacy rights as required by law. Members may contact us with questions regarding the collection, use, storage, or deletion of their personal data.

Right to Refuse Membership

Facture reserves the right to refuse, deny, revoke, or terminate membership and access to any individual at its sole discretion, with or without prior notice, where permitted by applicable law.

Membership may be refused or revoked for reasons including, but not limited to, violations of community rules, abusive or disruptive behavior, attempts to evade moderation actions, fraudulent activity, security concerns, or any conduct that may negatively impact the safety, integrity, or operation of the community.

Nothing in these policies shall be interpreted as guaranteeing membership, access, or continued participation within Facture. Access to Facture is a privilege, not a right.

Use of Slurs and Offensive Language

Facture permits the use of slurs and other offensive language in certain contexts. However, members are expected to use such language responsibly and with consideration for others.

The use of slurs or offensive terms for the purpose of harassing, threatening, degrading, intimidating, or expressing hatred toward an individual or group based on protected characteristics is strictly prohibited. Content that promotes discrimination, violence, or hostility against others may result in moderation action, including suspension or removal from the community.

Context matters, and moderation decisions will be made based on the intent, usage, and impact of the content in question.

Punishment: Warn depending on severity.

Language

English is the primary language of the Facture community. Members are encouraged to communicate in English whenever possible to facilitate moderation, support, and effective communication with staff and other community members.

The use of other languages is not prohibited; however, staff may be unable to provide assistance or moderation services for content that cannot be reasonably understood.

The distribution of malicious content is strictly prohibited within the Facture community. Members found distributing or promoting malicious content will be subject to immediate moderation action, including suspension or permanent removal from the community.
