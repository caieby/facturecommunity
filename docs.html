ID Verification Process

There are three ways to get ID verified.

Government ID Verification

  1. Step 1: Prepare a Valid Government-Issued ID. Before beginning the verification process, ensure that you have a valid government-issued photo identification document available. Acceptable forms of identification may include a driver’s license, state-issued ID card, REAL ID, passport, or other government-issued identification.
  2. Step 2: Obtain a Blank Sheet of Paper. Prepare a blank sheet of paper that will be used as part of the verification process. Ensure the paper is clean, legible, and large enough to display any required information clearly in a photograph.
  3. Step 3: Prepare Your Identification Document. Place your government-issued identification document on the sheet of paper. For your privacy and security, redact, cover, or blur all information on the ID except your Date of Birth (DOB). Ensure that the Date of Birth remains clearly visible and readable in the final photograph.
  4. Step 4: Record Your Account Information. On the same sheet of paper, clearly write your Discord username (not your display name) alongside your Date of Birth (DOB). Ensure that both pieces of information are legible and match the information associated with your account and identification document.
  5. Step 5: Submit Your Verification Request. Take a clear photograph showing the required verification materials. Once the photograph has been taken, open a support ticket and submit everything for review.

Roblox Verification

  1. Step 1: Open Roblox.
  2. Step 2: Press the gear icon in the top right, then click Settings.
  3. Step 3: Record a short video of your age range and whether you are ID verified or not — you must be ID verified on Roblox to be verified via this method. Refresh the page a few times in the video to confirm the website wasn’t tampered with, then open a support ticket and submit everything for review.

Cross Verification

You can only cross verify from servers we are partnered with.

  1. Step 1: Go to the server that we are partnered with, open your profile, and keep everything in view.
  2. Step 2: Take a screenshot of your profile, including your roles, so we can see if you have the verification role in that server.
  3. Step 3: Open a support ticket and submit everything for review.

Vfuse Verification Tutorial

For specific uploads, we now use vFuse to verify user accounts and prevent impersonation, exploiting, etc.

What Vfuse Can Access

  • Vfuse Discord Bot: Usernames, banners, profiles, etc. All account information accessed is only public information that can be seen by anyone.
  • Roblox Authenticator: Roblox user IDs, Roblox usernames, etc. All account information accessed is only public information that can be seen by anyone.

Verification Steps

  1. Step 1: Choose How You Want to Continue. Once you join the game, a menu will show up. For all new joiners, click “First time setup.”
  2. Step 2: Link Your Discord Account. Link your Discord account to our Discord bot — you must be in our server in order to do this. Click “Generate Discord link” and paste it into your browser of choice.
  3. Step 3: Authorize the Bot. Click “Authorize.” The bot can only access your username, avatar, and banner, and read your member info such as nickname, avatar, roles, etc. We do not collect personal information.
  4. Step 4: Save Your Auth Code. Copy your auth code and save it somewhere secure — this is your personal key to access the game. If you ever lose your personal auth key, open a support ticket in the Discord server and we can retrieve it for you.
  5. Step 5: Finish in Studio. Go back to your Studio application, click continue, and paste in your auth code. You’re in!

How to Use Outfit Changer (GOAT)

Follow these instructions closely to figure out how to use our inbuilt outfit changer.

  1. Step 1: Copy this URL into your browser (do not press enter yet): https://avatar.roblox.com/v1/users/[YOUR USER ID]/outfits
  2. Step 2: Replace [YOUR USER ID] with your Roblox user ID. You can get this by going to Roblox, going to your profile, and looking at the search box. You should see something like https://www.roblox.com/users/1234567890/profile — the number after “users/” needs to be copied; that is your user ID.
  3. Step 3 (Final Step): Go back to Step 1 and replace [YOUR USER ID] with your actual Roblox user ID (without the brackets). It should bring up a list of all of your outfit names and outfit IDs.
